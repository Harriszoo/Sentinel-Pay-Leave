The double-time rule (Art. 10.10.E)

  • 8 hours of voluntary OT in a pay period opens the door — doesn't have to be one shift
  • After that, every voluntary hour that pay period is 2x
  • Must be signed up on the volunteer list, or no 2x
  • Pay period = 2 weeks, Saturday to Friday. Resets every period.
  • Mandatory hours don't count toward the 8
